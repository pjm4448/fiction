# The Womb of the Black Serpent

## The Lotus Bride’s Last Gasp

High above it all trapped behind his own dead eyes what remained of Valthar watched every atrocity committed by the monster that had worn him as mask and womb forever forced to witness his own hands reaming fresh victims carving runes into bleeding flesh and feeding screaming souls to the serpent while the voices his mother’s the twins’ Nyxara’s and now his own stolen laughter echoed forever. A single tear of blood rolled down what used to be his cheek.

The third descent was complete.

The Black Serpent ruled.

## Epilogue

Decades melted into centuries beneath the shadow of the Black Serpent. What had once been the proud city of Khemi now existed only as the Brothel of Scales a living nightmare where every palace every street and every temple echoed with the endless wet sounds of violation. Ancient noble bloodlines that once commanded armies were reduced to naked broken stock bent daily over altars of black stone while thick coils rutted them without pause or mercy.

Suspended within a cage of living serpent bone high above the central pit hung the empty shell that had once been Valthar the Veiled. His eyes remained forever open forever aware. The Black Serpent had preserved just enough of his mind so he could witness every horror performed by the monster that now wore scraps of his screaming face like a crown of flesh.

And somewhere in the deepest coils of its new god body the Serpent remembered the barbarian it had broken last of all and it laughed with Valthar’s stolen voice. It occasionally stroked the cage of bone with a coil forcing Valthar’s preserved mind to relive the exact moment Gromm faltered.

The End.
